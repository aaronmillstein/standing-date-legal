# How to delete your Standing Date account

You can delete your Standing Date account and its data at any time, with or
without the app.

## Option 1: In the app (fastest)

1. Open Standing Date and go to the **Date Vault** tab.
2. Tap the **person icon** at the top right.
3. Tap **Delete account**.
4. Type **DELETE** and tap **Delete my account**.

Your account is deleted right away and you're signed out.

## Option 2: By email (no app needed)

Email **[standingdateapp@gmail.com](mailto:standingdateapp@gmail.com)** from the email address you signed up with, with
the subject "Delete my account". We'll reply to confirm and delete your
account within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm the request from that
address so no one else can delete your account.

## What gets deleted

- Your account: email address, name and password
- Your home city
- Your taste quiz answers and the history of how you responded to
  suggestions
- Your suggestion schedules, reviews and notes, and records of calendar
  events
- Your push notification token, sign-in sessions and invites
- Suggestions that were only for you

## What's kept

- **If you have a linked partner:** they keep their own account, your
  shared Date Vault ideas (including ones you added, without your name),
  the blogs and feeds you followed together, and suggestions made for them.
  Your link is removed.
- **If you don't:** your Date Vault and everything in it is deleted too.
- **Your phone's calendar:** events the app added stay in your calendar.
  Delete them in your calendar app if you don't want them.

Server logs that may include your account ID are deleted automatically
within about 30 days.
